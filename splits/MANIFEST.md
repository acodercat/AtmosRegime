## Rules

1. Only `train` (and its nested `train_*` subsets) may supply training data.
   A parent's numeric and paraphrase variants inherit its split — they leak the
   parent's method, so they never cross the boundary.
2. `probe_*` splits are measured before and after training and are never trained on.
3. Reporting accuracy on the full 436-problem core set is no longer meaningful once
   training has happened. Report per-split.

## Before comparing anything against `baselines.json`

Two of its numbers are known-stale and **neither is fixed** — the call is the researcher's,
not the tooling's. Recorded here so nobody quotes them by accident.

- **`cross_domain` predates the unit fix.** It was computed while 209 sub-answers carried an
  empty `unit`, which disables unit-aware grading. Recomputing from current upstream runs
  moves 8 model-domain cells, all upward, by up to 10.6 points (kimi-k2.6 ecology
  0.789 -> 0.895). A post-training number compared against the frozen baseline would show an
  "improvement" that is only a grading fix.
- **`trap_gap` mixes two trap sets.** It reads all of `experiments/trap/` upstream, which is
  the benchmark paper's 9-configuration sweep — and that sweep is not being completed here
  (decision 2026-09-07). Only deepseek-v4-flash, deepseek-v4-flash-reasoning and
  gpt55-reasoning hold the 264 pre-expansion ids (gpt55-reasoning run 3 holds 263, one
  `error` record); the other six hold the original 67 plus `error` placeholders. Pin it to
  the original 67 across all nine configurations, or to the 264 across the three complete
  ones, and say which.

Core-split and scaffolding baselines did not move when this was checked: 72 numbers, 0 changed.
